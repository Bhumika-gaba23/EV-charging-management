Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026. Written to meet India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Who we are

Pluggo is an EV charging app operated by the Pluggo team (the "data fiduciary"). Questions, requests and complaints: .

What we collect and why

DataWhy
Your mobile number (when you sign in)To verify you with a one-time SMS code and keep your charges and receipts on every device.
Charging sessions: station, connector, vehicle type, energy, cost, timesTo run and bill your charge, show your history and resolve disputes.
Payment references (Razorpay order and payment IDs, amounts, refunds)To confirm payment, issue receipts and process refunds. We never see or store card, UPI or bank details.
Preferences: chosen vehicle, saved stationsTo remember your choices.
Bookings: station, charger, time slot and whether you used itTo hold the charger for you and release it if you do not arrive.
Station ratings and commentsTo show other drivers how a station is. Others see the stars and comment, never your number.
Problem reports: station, charger, what went wrong and your noteSo the operator can fix the charger and tell you when it is done.
Wallet credit: amounts added or used, and whyTo apply credit to your next charges.
A random visitor cookieTo keep you signed in and protect against forged requests. No advertising or tracking cookies are used.

Your location stays on your phone. It is used to sort and search nearby stations; when you search real stations, your position rounded to about 100 m is sent to our server only to find stations near it, and is kept only in short-lived server logs. The trip planner sends your rounded starting point and destination city in the same way, only to find charging stops. If you turn on charging alerts, your phone schedules them itself; we do not use a push service. We do not sell personal data or use it for advertising.

Who processes data for us

  • Google Firebase Authentication: sends the SMS code and verifies your number (uses reCAPTCHA).
  • Razorpay: processes payments and refunds.
  • Our hosting and backup providers: run the server and store an encrypted copy of the database. Servers may be located outside India.
  • OpenStreetMap map tiles and, if you enable it, Google Maps: load map images and search results directly to your phone.

How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account we erase your number, preferences, history, bookings, ratings and wallet credit. Problem reports you sent are kept for the operator's maintenance records with your note removed and no link to you. Records of payments you made are kept as anonymous accounting entries, without your number, for as long as tax law requires (up to 8 years).

Delete your account

In the Pluggo app or at this website's Settings page, sign in and choose Delete my account and data. Your mobile number, saved stations, vehicle choice and charging history are erased immediately and your phone sign-in record is removed. Receipts for payments you made remain only as anonymous accounting entries, without your number, as tax law requires. If you cannot sign in, contact us using the details above and we will delete your account within 30 days.

Your rights

  • Access: your sessions and receipts are always visible in the app.
  • Erasure: Settings → Delete my account and data, at any time.
  • Correction and withdrawal of consent: sign out, or delete your account; you can also contact us.
  • Grievance: contact us using the details above; we reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you may approach the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. Sign-in tokens and payment signatures are verified on our server, sessions use HttpOnly cookies, and operator tools require a registered phone number.

Children

Pluggo is meant for adult vehicle owners. Do not sign in if you are under 18.

Changes

We will update the date above and show a notice in the app when this policy changes in a material way.
