The service

Pluggo, operated by the Pluggo team, lets you find EV charging stations, start and stop charging at Pluggo network chargers, and pay for the energy delivered. Stations shown from OpenStreetMap or Google are listed for information only; we do not operate them and cannot guarantee their details, prices or availability.

Your account

You sign in with a mobile number you own and are responsible for activity on your account. You must be 18 or older. You can delete your account at any time in Settings.

Charging and payment

  • You pay for the energy measured by the charger's meter at the tariff shown before you start. Your budget and target are limits: charging stops when either is reached.
  • Payments are processed by Razorpay. A new charge can start only after the previous one is paid.
  • If a charger fault means you were charged incorrectly, contact us; refunds go back to your original payment method, usually within 5–7 working days.
  • Estimates of range, time and cost are typical values, not guarantees.

Safe and fair use

Follow the instructions at the charger, use compatible equipment, and move your vehicle when charging ends. Do not misuse, damage or try to bypass chargers or the app.

Pilot features

Some Pluggo network stations may be simulated while partner chargers are connected, and payments may run in test mode, in which case no real money moves. The app shows when this applies.
