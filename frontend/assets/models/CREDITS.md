# Pluggo vehicle model credits

These models retain their original licences. Pluggo is an independent college
charging demonstration; model geometry does not establish a vehicle's battery
capacity, connector support or other specifications.

## Pluggo Cargo — procedural three-wheeler and cargo-poster.png

Original code-generated vehicle design in `../../cargo-model.js`, authored for
this project. Rounded enclosed cab, three wheels, ribbed delivery box, mirrors,
lights and original Pluggo branding. Static geometry is merged by material to
reduce draw calls. It represents a generic electric delivery three-wheeler, not a
specific manufacturer's vehicle. Battery and connector data are fictional demo
profiles. The poster is rendered from this same model. Three.js geometry utilities
retain the existing MIT attribution.
