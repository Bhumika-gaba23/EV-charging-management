Features

Everything a driver needs.
Nothing they don't.

Chargers near you

The Pluggo network plus 2,374 public chargers, sorted by distance. Filter by fast charging, CCS2, Type 2, CHAdeMO or 3-pin.

Trip planner

Pick a city and Pluggo finds safe charging stops on real roads, even through the hills, and tells you when to charge first.

Book and save

Reserve a charger slot for later and pay 20% less off-peak. Pluggo shows the saving before you start.

Live charging

Watch battery, energy and cost update live, and get a "charging complete" alert, even when the app is closed.

Pay, receipts, wallet

Pay with UPI or card through Razorpay. Receipts are saved to your number, and wallet credit pays automatically.

Green impact

See the CO₂, petrol and money you saved compared with a similar petrol vehicle, every month.

Find

The right charger, wherever you are.

Pluggo knows your town from GPS. Type any place in India, from Kasol to Kanyakumari, and see the nearest chargers that fit your plug, with directions in one tap.

Plan

Long trips, without range anxiety.

Every leg is checked with a 15% range buffer. Pluggo picks the fastest charger on the way and tells you how long each stop takes and what it costs.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Pluggo free?

Yes. The app is free. You pay only for the energy you charge, at the station's rate, through Razorpay.

Can I start any charger from the app?

In-app charging works at Pluggo network stations, which connect to us over OCPP. For other public chargers, Pluggo shows where they are, their plugs and power, and directions; you start and pay at the charger or in its operator's app.

Does it work on iPhone?

The Android app is available now. On iPhone, use the web app in Safari: it has the same features.

What happens to my data?

Your charges and receipts are saved to your mobile number when you sign in. Your location is rounded to about 100 m before any search. You can delete your account and data in Settings at any time.

Is this a pilot?

Yes. Pluggo network stations are simulated while partner chargers are onboarded, and payments run in Razorpay test mode, so no real money moves.
